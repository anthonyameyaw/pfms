Contributing Farms

This plant log allocates processing fees. Enter farm production in its harvest record to add oil to storage once.

For one farm, select its name; all own-farm gallons and processing fees go to that farm. For multiple farms, enter each farm's gallons (25 litres each); the quantities must equal the own-farm total. Bunch counts are optional. Leave all rows blank only if the farm is not yet known.
