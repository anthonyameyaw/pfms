① Cost Distribution — % of Gallon Value

For each harvest: what % of the gallon sale value went to labour, transport, and what remained as gross margin.

Labour Transport Net Margin
② Husks Processed per Gallon Produced

Efficiency metric — how many husks were needed to produce one gallon of palm oil. Lower = more efficient.

③ Levelized Cost per Gallon (GHS/gal)

Total cost (labour + transport) divided by gallons produced. Green = below GHS 20/gal, amber = 20–35, red = above 35.

④ Gross Margin per Gallon (GHS)

Sale price minus levelized cost per gallon. Shows how much you actually keep per gallon after production costs.

⑤ Absolute Cost Stack per Harvest (GHS)

Stacked bars showing the actual GHS amounts spent on harvest labour, threshing labour, and transport for each harvest.

⑥ Labour Return on Investment

GHS of income earned per GHS spent on labour. Higher = better labour efficiency.
