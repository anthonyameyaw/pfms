How these figures are calculated

Consolidated totals exclude transfers between your farms and processing plant. Oil purchases are expensed when bought; oil sales are revenue when recorded. Cost per gallon includes harvesting, defruiting, linked transport and processing fees for completed harvests. Combined cost is total harvest costs divided by their total gallons. Pruning, upkeep and unfinished harvest costs are excluded from this metric. Shared costs and incomplete records can affect your selling-price estimate.
