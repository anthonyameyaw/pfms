How totals work

Expected interest = principal × agreed return percentage. Total expected = principal + interest. Each investment keeps its own rate and due date. The next due date is the earliest unpaid obligation, including overdue dates. All recorded investments, including settled ones, are included in lifetime totals; repayments reduce outstanding balances.
